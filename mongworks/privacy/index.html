MongWorks

Privacy, data, and support information

MongWorks is a preventative bike-maintenance app. It projects a bike's component wear from a member's real riding data via Strava, and prompts service at the right time.

Data we collect

To do this, MongWorks collects and stores:

  • Strava data — via Strava's OAuth connection (scope profile:read_all, activity:read_all), we read your ride/activity data (distance, elevation, timing, and location of rides) and your registered gear (bike names), used to attribute wear to the correct bike.
  • Account data — your email address and authentication details, held by our backend provider (Supabase).
  • Service Log photos — optional documentation photos you attach when logging a service or inspection.
  • Postcode — used only to estimate shipping cost/time when comparing parts prices; not a full address, not device location.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising — MongWorks has no ad or analytics SDKs.

Requesting account deletion

To delete your MongWorks account and the data listed above, email rudi751@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, asking us to delete it.

Once we process the request, we will:

  • Revoke your Strava connection directly with Strava (not just forget it on our side)
  • Delete your Bikes, Components, Service Log, and Gear Mappings
  • Delete your account and sign-in details

Any Bike/Component catalog corrections you contributed that have already been shared with other members stay in place — they aren't personally identifiable, and removing them would break other members' bikes that rely on the same shared reference data.

Support

Using MongWorks with a connected Strava account and have a question or ran into a problem — with the Strava connection, your bikes, or anything else in the app? Email rudi751@gmail.com and we'll help.
